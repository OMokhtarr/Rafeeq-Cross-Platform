Delete your Rafeeq account

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your Rafeeq account and the data tied to it. You only have a Rafeeq account if you signed in with Google or Apple (for example, to subscribe to Rafeeq Pro). If you never signed in, there is no account to delete: your notes, bookmarks, and progress live only on your device, and uninstalling the App removes them.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. If you subscribed to Rafeeq Pro, cancel it in Google Play (or the App Store) first, or it will keep renewing. We cannot cancel a store subscription for you.

Delete it in the App

  1. Open Rafeeq and go to the Account page.
  2. In the Account section, where your name and email are shown, tap Delete account.
  3. Read "Delete your account?" and tap Delete account to confirm. (Tap Keep account to cancel instead.)
  4. Wait for "Deleting…" to finish. The App shows "Account deleted" and signs you out.

If deletion fails (for example, with no connection), the App says so, your account stays, and you can try again when you are online.

Or ask us by email

If you cannot open the App, email or.mokhtar@gmail.com from the email address on your Rafeeq account, with the subject "Delete my account". Mention whether you signed in with Google or Apple. We will confirm that the request comes from the account holder and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. It removes, in this order:

  1. Your Sign in with Apple grant, if we hold one. The App revokes it with Apple.
  2. Your subscription record in RevenueCat, the service that tracks Rafeeq Pro.
  3. Your Rafeeq account: your account ID, email address, name, and sign-in details, along with any data stored against the account.
  4. Data stored with your account: if you were a Rafeeq Pro subscriber with sync, the notes, bookmarks, memorisation (Hifz) progress, worship tracker entries, and settings synced to your account. This is removed together with the account, and nothing is kept after deletion.

If a step fails, the deletion stops there and the account stays, so you can try again; it is never left half-removed without you knowing.

What is not deleted

  • Your store subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions (on iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions). The store keeps its own purchase records under its own policy.
  • Data on your device: notes, bookmarks, progress, and settings (including copies that were synced) stay on the phone until you uninstall the App or clear its storage. Export a backup first from Account → Backup if you want to keep them.
  • Apple, if you signed in with Apple on Android. Apple accounts created through the Android app leave us no Apple token to revoke. Remove Rafeeq yourself in your Apple ID settings → Sign in with Apple.
