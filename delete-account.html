Delete Your Account and Data

App: Rafeeq (com.rafeeq.quranquiz) · Last updated: 8 August 2026

This page explains how to delete data associated with Rafeeq. You do not need to install the App to make a deletion request.

Rafeeq does not run its own account system. Signing in is optional and uses a Quran Foundation account, so account data is held by the Quran Foundation. The steps below cover both the data on your device and the data synced to that account.

1. Delete data stored on your device

Bookmarks, notes, memorisation progress, streaks, preferences, and cached Quran text and audio are stored only on your device. To erase them:

  1. Uninstall Rafeeq, or
  2. Open Android Settings → Apps → Rafeeq → Storage and tap Clear storage.

This is immediate and irreversible. No request to us is needed.

2. Sign out (removes account access from the device)

In the App, open Account and choose Sign out. This erases your sign-in tokens from the device.

Signing out does not delete data already synced to your Quran Foundation account — use step 3 for that.

3. Request deletion of synced account data

If you signed in, the following may be stored against your Quran Foundation account: your basic profile, reading streaks and daily activity, memorisation goals, and notes.

To request deletion, either:

  • Contact the Quran Foundation directly, since they are the account provider and hold this data — see quran.foundation; or
  • Email us at or.mokhtar@gmail.com with the subject "Rafeeq account deletion request" and the email address you signed in with. We will forward and follow up on your request.

We aim to acknowledge requests within 7 days. Deletion is completed by the Quran Foundation under their own data-retention policy.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Data Where it lives On deletion
Bookmarks, notes, Hifz progress, preferences Your device Erased immediately on uninstall or clear storage
Cached Quran text, translations, fonts, audio Your device Erased immediately on uninstall or clear storage
Sign-in tokens Your device Erased on sign-out or uninstall
Profile, streaks, goals, notes (if signed in) Quran Foundation Deleted on request, per their retention policy
Recite Mode audio Not stored Nothing to delete — audio is transcribed live and never saved by the App

Rafeeq does not operate its own servers for user data, keeps no backups of your account data, and shows no advertising.
