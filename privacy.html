Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026  ·  Effective: 14 August 2026

Rafeeq ("the App") is a Quran reader, memorisation aid, and quiz app. This policy explains exactly what data the App handles, what stays on your device, and what is sent elsewhere.

The App has no accounts and no sign-in, and needs no permissions for ordinary use. The one feature that sends data off your device — Recite Mode — is optional and is described below.

Rafeeq is an independent application. It uses the Quran Foundation APIs and displays Quran text, translations, tafsir, and recitation audio sourced from Quran.com. Rafeeq is not an official Quran Foundation application, and it is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or operated by the Quran Foundation. Questions about this App should come to the contact address at the bottom of this page, not to the Foundation.

1. Data stored on your device

The following are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us or to anyone else:

  • App preferences (language, night mode, mushaf style, font size)
  • Bookmarks, notes, and reading position
  • Memorisation (Hifz) plans, progress, and streaks
  • Quran text, translations, and fonts cached for offline reading
  • Downloaded recitation audio files

Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage from Android settings, removes all of this permanently.

2. Recite Mode and your microphone

Recite Mode sends your voice to a third party. This is the only feature that transmits audio, it is never on by default, and it runs only while you are actively using it.

Recite Mode listens to your recitation and follows along with the text. To turn speech into text, the App streams your microphone audio in real time to Deepgram, a speech-recognition provider based in the United States.

  • Audio is streamed live for transcription only. The App does not record your voice to a file, does not keep the audio, and does not send it anywhere else.
  • The transcribed text is used only to match your recitation against the verse on screen, and is discarded when the session ends.
  • Android asks for microphone permission the first time you use Recite Mode. If you decline, the rest of the App works normally.
  • Deepgram processes the audio under its own privacy policy and its retention settings. We do not use your recitation to train any model.

3. Religious information and your consent

Recitation audio, memorisation progress, and reading activity reveal religious practice, and many privacy laws treat that as a special category of sensitive personal data. Rafeeq treats it that way regardless of where you live.

  • Nothing sensitive is processed by default. Reading, memorisation, and quiz activity never leave your device at all, so no consent is needed for them — there is no collection to consent to.
  • Recite Mode is strictly opt-in. It is the only feature that transmits anything derived from your religious practice. It transmits only while you deliberately start a session, and it requires two separate affirmative acts: you must open Recite Mode yourself, and you must grant the Android microphone permission. Declining either one leaves every other part of the App fully usable.
  • You can withdraw that consent at any time by revoking the microphone permission in Android Settings → Apps → Rafeeq → Permissions, or simply by not using Recite Mode. Withdrawal takes effect immediately; because no audio is retained, there is nothing left to delete.

4. We do not train AI models on your content

Your notes, reflections, bookmarks, memorisation records, and recitation audio are never used to train, fine-tune, or evaluate any machine-learning model, by us or by anyone else, and they are never sold, mined, or repurposed for any feature beyond the ones described in this policy. We build no advertising or behavioural profiles of any kind. If that ever changes, it would require your separate, explicit opt-in consent first — continuing to use the App would not be treated as agreement.

5. No account required

The App has no sign-in and no user account. Your notes, bookmarks, memorisation progress, and your Hifz streak are written only to this device — they are never uploaded, never synced, and are not tied to your identity in any way.

6. Third parties the App connects to

These are every external service the App contacts, what each one is for, and what it receives. Each operates under its own privacy policy, linked below. None of them is given access to your notes, bookmarks, or memorisation data, because that data never leaves your device.

Service Purpose What it receives
Quran Foundation Quran text, translations, tafsir, recitation audio Which pages and verses the App requests. No account, no user identifier, and nothing that identifies you.
Cloudflare Hosts our token broker, a small service that holds our API credentials so they are never shipped inside the App Standard connection metadata such as your IP address, as with any web request. No content of yours passes through it.
Deepgram Speech recognition for Recite Mode Live microphone audio, only while Recite Mode is running
jsDelivr (CDN) Mushaf fonts Nothing beyond a standard web request

All connections use encrypted transport (HTTPS/WSS).

7. International data transfers

Deepgram and Cloudflare are based in the United States, and Cloudflare serves requests from data centres worldwide, so if you use Recite Mode outside the US your audio is processed there. The safeguards that apply: audio travels only over an encrypted connection (WSS), is transcribed in the moment and never stored by us, is never linked to an account or identity because the App has none, and reaches no third party beyond the transcription provider itself.

8. What the App does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, files, or call history

9. Security

The measures protecting the small amount of data that moves at all:

  • In transit: every connection uses TLS (HTTPS for API and font requests, WSS for the Recite Mode audio stream). The App makes no unencrypted network calls.
  • At rest: your notes, bookmarks, streaks, and cached Quran content are held in the App's private storage area, which Android isolates from other apps and encrypts at the OS level on any device with device encryption enabled. We operate no user database, so there is no server-side store of your data to protect.
  • Credentials: API keys and secrets are never bundled into the App package. They are held as encrypted secrets in the token broker, which returns only short-lived tokens — the Recite Mode token expires in about a minute. Secrets are rotated whenever a compromise is suspected and can be revoked immediately at the provider.
  • Access control: the token broker exposes only the two endpoints the App needs, and requests the minimum API scope required to read Quran content — no broader permission is requested or held.
  • Breach reporting: any actual or suspected unauthorised access, breach, or exposure of data involving the Quran Foundation APIs will be reported to the Quran Foundation within 24 hours of discovery, alongside any notification the law requires us to give you.

10. Backing up your data

Because there is no account, the App gives you a file instead. In Account → Backup, "Export My Data" writes your notes, bookmarks and streaks to a JSON file you control; "Restore From File" loads it back on another device. The file stays wherever you save it — the App never uploads it anywhere.

11. Accessing, correcting, and deleting your data

Access and correction. Everything the App holds about you is on your device and directly visible in the App — your notes, bookmarks, memorisation progress, and streaks can be read, edited, or removed individually at any time in the App itself. "Export My Data" in Account → Backup gives you the whole set as a portable file.

Deletion. Uninstall the App, or clear its storage from Android Settings → Apps → Rafeeq → Storage. Deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no account to close, no server-side copy, and no request to send us. If you want to keep your notes and streaks first, export them from Account → Backup before uninstalling.

Why the usual account-deletion and OAuth-revocation steps do not apply here. Rafeeq has no sign-in, does not use Quran Foundation user accounts, and requests no OAuth permission from you, so there is no authorisation to revoke and no linked account whose deletion could affect your data. We operate no user database and hold no copy of your content on any server, which means there is nothing for us to delete on request and nothing of yours in any backup of ours. Should Rafeeq ever add an account system, we would honour deletion requests by permanently deleting the data within 30 days and purging it from backups within 90 days, and deleting your Quran Foundation account would delete the associated data held here — but today no such data exists.

12. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Recite Mode requires microphone permission, which on most devices requires the device owner to grant it.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially — particularly regarding what data leaves your device — the "Last updated" date above will change and the change will be described in the app listing's release notes. Where a change would involve newly collecting or transmitting sensitive religious information, we will ask for your explicit consent before it takes effect rather than relying on your continued use of the App.
