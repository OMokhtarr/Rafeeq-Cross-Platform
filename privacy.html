Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026  ·  Effective: 29 October 2026

Rafeeq ("the App") is a Quran reader, memorisation aid, and quiz app. This policy explains exactly what data the App handles, what stays on your device, and what is sent elsewhere.

No account is required for the free app, and it needs no permissions for Quran reading, memorisation, or quizzes. Signing in with Google or Apple is optional, and is needed only to subscribe to Rafeeq Pro (sections 4 and 7). Two optional features ask for a permission and can send limited data off your device: Recite Mode (microphone) and prayer times / qibla (approximate location). Recite Mode is part of the optional Rafeeq Pro subscription, which involves the purchase and account data described in sections 4 and 7. All of these are described below.

Rafeeq is an independent application. It uses the Quran Foundation APIs and displays Quran text, translations, tafsir, and recitation audio sourced from Quran.com. Rafeeq is not an official Quran Foundation application, and it is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or operated by the Quran Foundation. Questions about this App should come to the contact address at the bottom of this page, not to the Foundation.

1. Data stored on your device

The following are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us or to anyone else, except that if you are a signed-in Rafeeq Pro subscriber, a copy of your notes, bookmarks, memorisation progress and settings is also kept in your account (section 7):

  • App preferences (language, night mode, mushaf style, font size)
  • Bookmarks, notes, and reading position
  • Memorisation (Hifz) plans, progress, and streaks
  • Quran text, translations, and fonts cached for offline reading
  • Downloaded recitation audio files
  • Your approximate location and the place name derived from it, used for prayer times, qibla, and the home-screen widget
  • Prayer-time settings (calculation method, which times are shown, notification choices) and widget appearance settings
  • Worship tracker entries, such as prayers and fasts you tick off
  • Favourite azkar
  • Saved quiz range sets

Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage from Android settings, removes all of this permanently.

2. Recite Mode and your microphone

Recite Mode sends your voice to a third party. This is the only feature that transmits audio, it is never on by default, and it runs only while you are actively using it.

Recite Mode listens to your recitation and follows along with the text. To turn speech into text, the App streams your microphone audio in real time to the speech-recognition provider you choose in Settings: either Deepgram (the default) or Soniox. Both are based in the United States. Only the provider you selected receives audio.

  • Audio is streamed live for transcription only. The App does not record your voice to a file, does not keep the audio, and does not send it anywhere else.
  • The transcribed text is used only to match your recitation against the verse on screen, and is discarded when the session ends.
  • Android asks for microphone permission the first time you use Recite Mode. If you decline, the rest of the App works normally.
  • The provider processes the audio under its own privacy policy. Soniox states that real-time requests are processed transiently and not stored; Deepgram applies its own retention settings. We do not use your recitation to train any model.

3. Location, prayer times, and qibla

Prayer times, the qibla compass, prayer notifications, and the home-screen widget need to know roughly where you are. Android asks for approximate (coarse) location permission the first time you use them. The App never requests precise location.

  • Calculated on your device. Prayer times and the qibla direction are computed on the device from your coordinates. Your location is not sent to us or to any prayer-times service.
  • Place name lookup. To show the name of your town or city, the App passes your approximate coordinates to Android's built-in geocoding service. On most devices this service is provided by Google and performs a network request under Google's privacy policy. The result is cached on your device so the lookup is not repeated unless you move.
  • Compass. The qibla compass reads the device's motion and magnetic sensors. These readings are used only on the device and are never stored or transmitted.
  • Notifications and alarms. If you turn on prayer notifications, the App asks for notification permission and schedules exact alarms on the device. It also restores them after a restart or a time-zone change. No push service or server is involved.
  • Declining or withdrawing. If you decline location permission, or revoke it in Android Settings → Apps → Rafeeq → Permissions, the rest of the App works normally. Prayer times and qibla will not be available until a location is provided. Clearing the App's storage removes the saved location.

4. Rafeeq Pro subscription

Recite Mode is offered as an optional paid subscription, Rafeeq Pro. The rest of the App stays free and works without it. Subscriptions are bought through Google Play (or the App Store on iPhone) and managed for us by RevenueCat, a subscription service based in the United States.

  • Linked to your account. Rafeeq Pro belongs to your Rafeeq account (section 7). When you are signed in, RevenueCat identifies you by your Rafeeq account ID, so the subscription follows the account to a new phone. Before you sign in, RevenueCat gives the installation a random anonymous ID instead, which is not linked to your name or email address. Either ID is shown as your Support ID in Account → Subscription, so you can quote it if you contact us about a purchase.
  • Purchase records. RevenueCat receives the store's purchase details — which plan, its price and currency, purchase and renewal dates, and the store's purchase token — together with your account ID (or the anonymous ID) and basic device information (device type and operating system). It uses them to confirm that a subscription is active, to restore it on a new phone, and to show it in the subscription management screen.
  • Paywall views. RevenueCat records when the subscription screen is shown or closed, so we can see whether it is clear. These records carry only that ID.
  • Payments. Google Play (or Apple) takes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Neither we nor RevenueCat ever see your card or payment details.
  • Subscription check. When you start Recite Mode, the App sends your sign-in token to our token broker, which verifies it with Supabase's public signing keys and asks RevenueCat whether that account's subscription is active before issuing a speech token. The answer may be remembered for a few minutes; nothing else is stored.
  • Restoring on a new phone. Sign in to the same Rafeeq account and your subscription is there. "Restore purchases" asks the store which subscriptions belong to the store account signed in on the device, and links them to your account.

5. Religious information and your consent

Recitation audio, memorisation progress, and reading activity reveal religious practice, and many privacy laws treat that as a special category of sensitive personal data. Rafeeq treats it that way regardless of where you live.

  • Nothing sensitive is processed by default. Reading, memorisation, quiz, azkar, and worship tracker activity (including the prayers and fasts you record) stay on your device unless you are a signed-in Rafeeq Pro subscriber, in which case a copy is kept in your account so your phones stay in step (section 7). For everyone else nothing leaves the device, so there is no collection to consent to.
  • Recite Mode is strictly opt-in. It is the only feature that transmits anything derived from your religious practice. It transmits only while you deliberately start a session, and it requires two separate affirmative acts: you must open Recite Mode yourself, and you must grant the Android microphone permission. Declining either one leaves every other part of the App fully usable.
  • You can withdraw that consent at any time by revoking the microphone permission in Android Settings → Apps → Rafeeq → Permissions, or simply by not using Recite Mode. Withdrawal takes effect immediately; because no audio is retained, there is nothing left to delete.
  • A Rafeeq Pro purchase record shows only that a subscription was bought and when. It contains nothing about what you recite, read, or memorise, and your account holds nothing of that either.

6. We do not train AI models on your content

Your notes, reflections, bookmarks, memorisation records, and recitation audio are never used to train, fine-tune, or evaluate any machine-learning model, by us or by anyone else, and they are never sold, mined, or repurposed for any feature beyond the ones described in this policy. We build no advertising or behavioural profiles of any kind. If that ever changes, it would require your separate, explicit opt-in consent first — continuing to use the App would not be treated as agreement.

7. Accounts and sign-in

No account is required for the free app. Reading, memorisation, quizzes, prayer times, and everything else except Rafeeq Pro work without signing in. A Rafeeq account is needed only to subscribe to Rafeeq Pro, so that the subscription belongs to you rather than to one phone.

  • How you sign in. With Google (on Android), or with Apple (on iPhone, and on Android where it is offered). Google or Apple tells us your name and email address; if you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive Apple's relay address instead of your real one. We never see your Google or Apple password.
  • What we hold. Your account ID, the email address and name from your sign-in provider, and the sign-in method. On iPhone, when you sign in with Apple, we also keep the token Apple issues for the app, only so that it can be revoked if you delete your account. If you are not a Rafeeq Pro subscriber, your notes, bookmarks, and other personal data are not part of your account; they stay on your device (section 1). Rafeeq Pro subscribers also have synced data, described next.
  • What syncs (Rafeeq Pro only). While you are signed in and subscribed to Rafeeq Pro, the App keeps a copy of the following in your account: notes; bookmarks; hidden verses; memorisation (Hifz) plans, progress, session counts, streak days, and freezes; recitation history; worship tracker entries and settings; favourite azkar; saved quiz range sets; and settings such as theme, language, your last page, and recent searches. This is the same set as the backup file in section 12. It does not include your subscription, your location, downloaded tafsirs or audio, cached Quran content, or the App's own bookkeeping. It is sent over an encrypted connection, only for the App to restore and combine your data across your phones, and used for nothing else.
  • Who syncs. Only signed-in Rafeeq Pro subscribers. Without a subscription, or when signed out, nothing is synced.
  • How phones are combined. When you sign in on a new phone, the data on the phone and the data in your account are combined; if the same item was changed on two phones, the most recent edit wins; and the first sync deletes nothing.
  • Where it is held. Accounts are run by Supabase, which hosts them in the [REGION] region.
  • How long. Until you delete your account. Signing out does not delete it. If your Rafeeq Pro subscription ends, syncing stops but the synced data stays in your account until you delete your account.
  • Deleting it. In the App, Account → Delete account, or by email. See section 13 and how to delete your account.

9. International data transfers

Deepgram, Soniox, RevenueCat, and Cloudflare are based in the United States, and Cloudflare serves requests from data centres worldwide, so if you use Recite Mode outside the US your audio is processed there, and a Rafeeq Pro purchase record is held there. Your Rafeeq account, if you create one, and the data synced to it for Rafeeq Pro subscribers, is held by Supabase in the [REGION] region. The safeguards that apply to audio: audio travels only over an encrypted connection (WSS), is transcribed in the moment and never stored by us, is not linked to your account (it is sent under a short-lived token and carries no account ID), and reaches no third party beyond the transcription provider itself.

The place-name lookup described in section 3 is handled by your device's geocoding provider, which may also process the coordinates outside your country. Only approximate coordinates are sent, and nothing that identifies you is sent with them.

10. What the App does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking SDKs. RevenueCat's records of subscription-screen views (section 4) are the only usage records
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No access to your precise location, and no location tracking in the background: approximate location is used only for prayer times and qibla
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, or call history

11. Security

The measures protecting the small amount of data that moves at all:

  • In transit: every connection uses TLS (HTTPS for API and font requests, WSS for the Recite Mode audio stream). The App makes no unencrypted network calls.
  • At rest: your notes, bookmarks, streaks, and cached Quran content are held in the App's private storage area, which Android isolates from other apps and encrypts at the OS level on any device with device encryption enabled. The only user data we hold on a server is your Rafeeq account (section 7), if you create one, plus the synced data of Rafeeq Pro subscribers, which is held by Supabase, accessible only to us and our hosting provider (Supabase), and encrypted in transit.
  • Credentials: API keys and secrets are never bundled into the App package. They are held as encrypted secrets in the token broker, which returns only short-lived tokens — the Recite Mode token expires in about a minute. Secrets are rotated whenever a compromise is suspected and can be revoked immediately at the provider.
  • Access control: the token broker exposes only the endpoints the App needs, and requests the minimum API scope required to read Quran content — no broader permission is requested or held. Its RevenueCat key is a server-side secret and never leaves the broker.
  • Breach reporting: any actual or suspected unauthorised access, breach, or exposure of data involving the Quran Foundation APIs will be reported to the Quran Foundation within 24 hours of discovery, alongside any notification the law requires us to give you.

12. Backing up your data

Unless you are a Rafeeq Pro subscriber with sync (section 7), your notes and progress are not stored in your account, so the App gives you a file for backing them up. In Account → Backup, "Export My Data" writes your data to a JSON file you control; "Restore From File" loads it back on another device. The file stays wherever you save it — the App never uploads it anywhere.

The backup file includes your notes, bookmarks, memorisation progress, streaks, preferences, worship tracker entries, favourite azkar, and saved quiz range sets. It does not include your saved location, which the App simply asks for again on the new device, or your Rafeeq Pro subscription, which comes back when you sign in to your account on the new device (or with "Restore purchases").

If you restore a backup on a phone that syncs, it adds to and overwrites what is there but never deletes anything, and the result then syncs to your account.

13. Accessing, correcting, and deleting your data

Access and correction. Everything the App holds about you is on your device and directly visible in the App — your notes, bookmarks, memorisation progress, and streaks can be read, edited, or removed individually at any time in the App itself. For Rafeeq Pro subscribers, edits and removals sync to your account. "Export My Data" in Account → Backup gives you the whole set as a portable file.

Deletion. Uninstall the App, or clear its storage from Android Settings → Apps → Rafeeq → Storage. Deletion is immediate and permanent — for the data held on your device there is no server-side copy and no request to send us. If you synced it as a Rafeeq Pro subscriber, uninstalling does not remove the copy in your account; delete your account to remove it. If you want to keep your notes and streaks first, export them from Account → Backup before uninstalling.

Your Rafeeq account. If you created an account, uninstalling the App does not delete it. Delete it in the App with Account → Delete account, or email or.mokhtar@gmail.com from the address on the account and we will delete it within 30 days. Deleting it permanently removes your account (including your email address and name), everything synced to it (notes, bookmarks, memorisation progress, worship tracker, and settings), and your RevenueCat subscription record, and, on iPhone, revokes the app's Sign in with Apple grant. If you signed in with Apple through the Android app, we hold no Apple token for you; you can remove Rafeeq from your Apple ID settings (Sign in with Apple) yourself. Full steps are on the account deletion page.

Subscription records. If you bought Rafeeq Pro, the purchase record is held by RevenueCat and by the store, so uninstalling does not remove it. Deleting your account deletes the RevenueCat record. If you never created an account, email us your Support ID (Account → Subscription) and we will delete it within 30 days. Cancel the subscription in Google Play (or the App Store) first — deleting your account or the record does not cancel it or stop billing. The store keeps its own record of the purchase under its privacy policy.

Quran Foundation accounts. Rafeeq does not use Quran Foundation user accounts and requests no OAuth permission from you, so there is no Quran Foundation authorisation to revoke. Your notes, bookmarks, and other content are not held on any server unless you are a Rafeeq Pro subscriber with sync, in which case they are removed with your account. Apart from your Rafeeq account, its synced data and your subscription record described above, there is nothing else for us to delete on request.

14. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Recite Mode requires microphone permission, and prayer times require location permission. On most devices, the device owner has to grant these. Rafeeq Pro can only be bought through the store account on the device, so purchases follow the store's own parental controls.

15. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially — particularly regarding what data leaves your device — the "Last updated" date above will change and the change will be described in the app listing's release notes. Where a change would involve newly collecting or transmitting sensitive religious information, we will ask for your explicit consent before it takes effect rather than relying on your continued use of the App.
